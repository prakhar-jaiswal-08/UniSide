# UniSide

### Your campus. Your community. Your marketplace.

UniSide is a student-focused platform that brings the everyday college community into one place.

**Buy. Sell. Discover. Connect.**

🌐 **Live:** [uniside.in](https://uniside.in)

---

## What is UniSide?

College communities already have everything a marketplace needs.

Students sell things they no longer need, look for affordable products, offer services, search for roommates, share updates, and connect with each other.

The problem is that all of it is scattered across WhatsApp groups, Instagram pages, and different communities.

**UniSide brings it together.**

---

## Features

### 📰 Campus Feed

A community feed built around student life.

- Share and discover campus content
- Explore posts from the student community
- Stay updated with what's happening around campus
- Discover activity beyond the marketplace

### 🛍️ Marketplace

A dedicated marketplace for students.

- Create listings
- Upload product images
- Browse products
- Search and sort
- Categories
- Product details
- Seller information
- Edit and manage your listings

### 🛠️ Services

Discover useful services offered by students within the community.

- Create service listings
- Browse services
- Discover student skills
- Connect with service providers

### 🏠 Roommates

Find students looking for roommates and accommodation.

- Create roommate listings
- Browse available listings
- Search and filter
- View preferences and details
- Manage availability

### ❤️ Wishlist

Save products you are interested in and find them later without searching again.

### 💬 Real-time Chat

Talk directly with other students through built-in messaging.

- Start conversations from listings
- Real-time messages
- Conversation list
- Message timestamps
- Auto-scroll
- Persistent conversations
- Responsive mobile experience
- Desktop split-chat layout

### 👤 Profiles

Student profiles provide context around the people you interact with while keeping private information protected.

### 🚨 Reports

Built-in reporting infrastructure for handling inappropriate listings and user activity.

---

## 🔐 College-focused

UniSide is designed around college communities.

Students sign up using their college email and verify their account before accessing the platform.

Authentication is handled through **Supabase Auth** and verification emails are delivered using **Resend**.
