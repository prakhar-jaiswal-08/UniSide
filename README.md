# UniSide

### Your campus. Your community. Your marketplace.

UniSide is a student-focused platform built to bring the everyday college community into one place.

Buy and sell things. Discover services. Find roommates. Share what's happening around campus. Save listings. Connect with other students in real time.

Instead of jumping between WhatsApp groups, Instagram pages, and random student communities, UniSide puts it all together.

**Live:** https://uniside.in

---

## 🧭 What is UniSide?

College life creates its own small ecosystem.

Students constantly need to:

- Sell things they no longer need
- Find affordable products from other students
- Discover useful student services
- Find roommates
- Ask questions and share updates
- Connect with people around them

UniSide is built around that ecosystem.
